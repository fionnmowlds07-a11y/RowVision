    Keys and legend

    Space plays and pauses. Left and Right step one frame, with Shift ten. C and F jump to the next catch or finish, with Shift the previous one. L hides the limbs and A the angles. Plus, Minus, 0 and Z control zoom. Scroll or pinch to zoom, and drag to move around. Left side, right side. Dashed lines are joints the model was unsure of, or legs filmed from an angle.
