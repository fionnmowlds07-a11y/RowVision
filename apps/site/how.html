From a phone clip to what to fix.

There is nothing to fit to the boat and nothing to wear. You film an outing the way you already do, and RowVision does the watching.

  1. Choose a clip

    Any phone video of the boat will do. If it is long, pick the 60 seconds you want looked at.

  2. Tap yourself

    On a clear frame, tap the rower who is you. Say which boat it is, whether you are on stroke side, bow side or sculling, and which seat.

  3. Leave it to run

    The analysis runs on your own computer. You can queue more clips while it works.

  4. Read the result

    You get an accuracy rating for the footage, your scores at the catch and the finish, and what to work on next.

  5. Check any number

    Open a measure to see the exact frames it came from, beside the target it was scored against.

  6. Step through the stroke

    Move frame by frame with the keyboard, with your joints and angles drawn over the picture.

  7. Look back

    Your past clips stay in your library, so you can see whether last month’s fix has held.

Filming that measures well.

  • Film from the side, level with the boat if you can.
  • Get close enough that the rower you care about is easy to pick out.
  • Keep them in the frame for at least a few full strokes.
  • Any boat works, from a single to an eight. In a crew, RowVision follows the one seat you tapped.

It says how far to trust it.

Every result carries an accuracy rating, marked as estimated. When the footage is poor, the overall score stays hidden until you have read the warning. When the rower is too small in the frame to measure, RowVision gives no score at all.

Film closer. No score given.

RowVision gives coaching feedback, not medical advice.
