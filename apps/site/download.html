Get RowVision.

A free desktop app. Your video is analysed on your own computer and stays there.

Download for Linux Windows install coming soon

Early preview. Results are still being checked against real crews, so treat the numbers as a guide.

Installing on Linux.

Download the AppImage, make it runnable, then open it:

chmod +x RowVision-*.AppImage
./RowVision-*.AppImage

What you will need.

  • A Windows or Linux computer.
  • A phone video of your boat, filmed from the side.
  • To know which seat you were in.

Before you start.

Film your next outing anyway. Side on, close enough to pick yourself out, for a few full strokes. Then open it in RowVision.

The filming guide shows what measures well, and where it is now says what is left to build.
