The app.

Your clips
Analysed and kept on your own computer. We never receive them or the results. Delete a clip in the app and it is gone.
One download
The first time you open it, the app fetches its measuring models from OpenMMLab, or Hugging Face if that fails. Like any download, they see your IP address. Nothing about your clips is sent.
People in your clips
You choose what to film and who sees it, so ask them first.

This website.

Who is responsible
RowVision, a free, non-commercial project made by a rower in Ireland. Contact privacy@rowvision.app.
Visiting
Cloudflare hosts the site and handles your IP address to deliver it and keep it safe. Our reason is our legitimate interest in running a secure website. We set no cookies, use no analytics and keep no record of your IP address.
Downloads
We count downloads by time, file and country. The count can’t identify you, and we keep it as long as it is useful.
Emailing us
We use your message to answer you, which is our legitimate interest, and delete it when it is dealt with. Mail passes through Cloudflare and Gmail.
Rowers on this site
The people in our clips and photos agreed to be shown, and we keep them up while they do. Any of them can change their mind by emailing us, and we will take it down.
Outside Europe
Cloudflare and Google may handle data outside Europe, under the safeguards EU law requires.

Your rights.

You can ask to see, correct, delete, limit or move the personal data we hold about you, object to our using it, or take back your agreement. Email privacy@rowvision.app. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission.

RowVision is for adults. It does not identify people or make decisions about them.
