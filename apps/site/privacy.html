Your video never leaves your computer.

RowVision is a desktop app. The models that find your joints run on your own machine, so there is nothing to upload and no one else to trust with your footage.

Where the analysis runs
On your computer, start to finish. The app fetches its measuring models once, the first time you open it, and after that it needs no internet connection.
Where your clips are kept
In a folder on your own disk, with the results beside them. Delete a clip in the app and it is gone.
Accounts
None. The desktop app has no sign-in.
Who is in the picture
Rowing footage shows other people, and often juniors. Because nothing is uploaded, filming your crew for your own review stays between you and them.
Downloads
When you download the app, the site notes the time, which file and your country, to count downloads. Nothing that could identify you is kept: no IP address, no cookies.
This website
The rowers in the clips on this site agreed to be in them.

What RowVision is not.

  • It is not a medical tool. It gives coaching feedback on technique.
  • It does not identify anyone. It follows the person you tap, by where they sit.
  • It is not for under-18s yet. That needs parental consent and a safeguarding review first.
