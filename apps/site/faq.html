Questions rowers ask first.

What do I need?

A Windows or Linux computer and a phone video of your boat. Nothing is fitted to the boat and there is nothing to wear.

What does it cost?

The desktop app is free.

Can I use it now?

Not yet. It is in a private preview while the installers are finished. See where it is now.

Does my video get uploaded?

No. The analysis runs on your own computer and the video stays there. More on your video.

Which boats does it work on?

Any, from a single to an eight, sculling or sweep, coxed or not. In a crew you tap yourself once and say your seat. Boats and crews.

How should I film?

From beside the boat, close enough to pick yourself out, for at least five full strokes. The filming guide.

How accurate is it?

It depends on the footage, so every result says. The rating is the share of angle readings expected to fall within 5° of the truth, and it is marked as estimated. How far to trust a result.

Why did I get no score?

Either you were too small in the picture, or most of the clip was filmed from in front of or behind the boat. RowVision gives no score when it cannot stand behind one.

Where do the targets come from?

They are RowVision’s reading of published rowing studies, most of them done on rowing machines. They have not yet been reviewed by a coach, and you can move any of them.

Why are knee angles not scored?

They depend on your proportions and are the hardest joint to read from a moving launch, so they are shown as a rough guide only.

Does it measure the blade?

No. It reads the body, not the oar.

Can I print my results?

Yes. The app saves two kinds of A4 PDF: photos of every catch and finish with the angles drawn on, and, from version 0.3.0, a statistics sheet with every number for the rowers you choose. See both reports.

Can my coach use it?

Anyone can run a clip, measure a whole crew and print the sheets. Accounts for coaches are planned for later.

Is it medical advice?

No. It is coaching feedback on technique.
